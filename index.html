Location & Transport

Urban Connectivity & Easy Accessibility.

Our campus is strategically located in the urban heart of Khanpur, Mahisagar, ensuring seamless connectivity for students from all nearby towns and cities.

Full Transport Available

Dedicated college bus services covering major routes across Mahisagar district. Regular GSRTC buses available from Khanpur Bus Stand.

Prime Urban Location

Located on the main highway road, easily accessible by auto-rickshaws, taxis, and private vehicles. Safe and secure neighborhood.

Ample Parking Space

Spacious parking facilities for two-wheelers and four-wheelers within the campus premises.
